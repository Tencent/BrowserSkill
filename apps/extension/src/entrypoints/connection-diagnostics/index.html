Connection diagnostics

Recording is off by default. Start before observing a connection problem, then close this page. After a failure, export the log before toggling the connection or opening worker DevTools.

Up to 2048 connection events are stored locally, without page content, URLs, tokens or RPC payloads. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Recording can affect worker scheduling; an empty log does not prove that the background was inactive.

Reading and exporting only inspect stored data. Start, stop and clear are explicit controls that may wake the background worker. Stop preserves the log; clear removes diagnostic events.
